# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 10, 2026**

Welcome to **yuvansh.info**.

This Privacy Policy explains how this website handles information when you visit it.

## 1. Information We Collect

**yuvansh.info does not intentionally collect, request, or store personal information from visitors.**

The website is a personal portfolio created to showcase projects, experiments, interests, and other work.

There are no user accounts, registration forms, payment forms, or other features on this website that are designed to collect personal information.

## 2. Information You Provide

You are not required to provide personal information to browse this website.

If you choose to contact me through an external service or website linked from yuvansh.info, that service may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy. Those services are separate from yuvansh.info.

## 3. Cookies and Tracking

yuvansh.info does not intentionally use cookies or tracking technologies to identify visitors or create personal profiles.

If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what technologies are used and why.

## 4. Third-Party Websites

This website may contain links to other websites, platforms, or services.

I do not control the privacy practices, security, or content of those third-party websites. Visiting an external website is subject to that website's own privacy policy and terms.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Because yuvansh.info does not intentionally collect personal information, visitors are not required to provide personal information to use the website.

## 6. Data Security

Because yuvansh.info does not intentionally collect or maintain a database of visitors' personal information, there is no personal-information database operated by this website.

Reasonable measures may still be taken to keep the website and its hosting environment secure.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a new **"Last Updated"** date.

## 8. Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of yuvansh.info, you can contact me through the contact method provided on the website.

---

**In short:** yuvansh.info is a portfolio website and does not intentionally collect or store personal information from visitors.
